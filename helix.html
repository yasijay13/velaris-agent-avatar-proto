Helix avatar as a state machine

Two interwoven strands built from one parametric curve, offset by half a turn, crossing three times per loop. Each vertex's depth sets its size and brightness — a cheap pseudo-3D that still reads as one continuous twisted ribbon. Two energy nodes per strand travel the curve's own parameter independently of the ribbon's rotation.

    Both strands are the same curve, x=cos(θ), y=sin(θ·wind+φ), z=sin(θ), one with φ=0 and one with φ=π — a single formula, not two authored shapes. The whole ribbon turns by phase·τ added into θ, an integer multiple of the loop so it closes exactly. Energy nodes advance along θ on their own schedule, independent of the ribbon's turn, which is why they slide along the strand instead of riding fixed to it. jitter (failed only) perturbs each vertex off its curve.
