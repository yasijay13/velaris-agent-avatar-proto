Signal avatar as a state machine

A hub node and seven satellites on fixed spokes. A pulse launches from the hub along each spoke on its own offset — evenly staggered around the same 3-second loop — and the satellite flashes on arrival. Nothing travels between satellites directly; every signal passes through the centre, like a relay.

Event log

    Each spoke's pulse position is (phase + i/7) % 1 — a fixed per-spoke offset, so all seven are just one shared clock sampled at different points, never independent timers to fall out of sync. Brightness envelopes as sin(local·π), which is zero at both ends of the spoke — that's what hides the seam where each pulse's cycle restarts. jitter (failed only) knocks satellites off their fixed angle and drops spoke continuity.
