Event log

    Points live on the six faces of a cube; back-facing faces cull out as the lattice turns, which is what reads as rotation rather than a flat sprite spin. wave is the coherent diagonal sweep across the whole lattice; twinkle is per-particle star flicker, each point running its own randomized frequency and phase so nothing repeats or syncs; drift is slow independent floating motion layered on top of the rigid rotation, always on, distinct from jitter which is instability reserved for the failed state. Brightness also shifts colour temperature — hot particles desaturate toward white, dim ones sit in the base hue — which is what makes a pop read as a spark rather than a dot getting bigger. hue is the only thing that should ever be treated as a brand decision.
