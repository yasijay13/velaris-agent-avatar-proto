    waveAmp deforms a fixed 12×12 x/z grid vertically through a periodic sum of sine/cosine terms — a saddle, a diagonal wave, a ridge, layered together on a 3-second loop; the grid positions themselves never drift or scatter. energy scales an entirely separate field: one or two Gaussian hotspots travelling their own closed path across the same grid, independent of the geometry, which is what actually drives brightness — not particle height. Colour runs a fixed dark-red → red → orange → cream → white-hot ramp by brightness. Trails are the particle's own last few projected positions, sampled and thinned into discrete dots, not a synthetic streak. jitter (failed only) displaces particles off their grid slot — a broken lattice, the one state kept intentionally incoherent against the coherence of every other state.
