    Each ridge is a sum of a few sine terms at different spatial frequencies and amplitudes — a cheap Fourier series, not a single sine — which is what keeps the silhouette irregular and organic instead of one clean repeating hump. Every term also travels in time at its own small integer speed, so the ridge keeps shifting even when the "speech" envelope is quiet. That envelope is a handful of raised-cosine humps placed around the loop, gated by breath; it multiplies every layer at once, so all four ridges surge and settle together like one voice. jitter (failed only) breaks individual samples off the curve.
