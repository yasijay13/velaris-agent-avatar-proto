    The core is a true icosahedron — 12 vertices at the golden ratio, edges found by nearest-neighbour distance rather than hand-listed — slowly turning on its own axis. Two large rings, tilted on very different axes, cross through it; each tilt oscillates as tilt + precessAmp·sin(phase·k), an integer k so it stays seamless across the loop, and the small amplitude is what reads as "precession" rather than spin. spread scales every ring together; jitter (failed only) knocks vertices and rings off their calculated positions.
